---
title: I wouldn't get a Porsche, I would get a DeWalt Roadster
---

Those who know me know I am on my second electronic vehicle and the more I see companies that make sports cars make expensive electronic vehicles I think to myself "where is the pedigree?"

Porsche, Lotus, Ferrari have a lot of experience in making tuned, engines with gearboxes which honestly would not translate well into the electric motor side.  

If you ask me who makes a decent electric motor then I would answer DeWalt, Milwaukee, Bosch etc - maybe it is time for them to get into the car industry!
